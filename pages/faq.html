Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Telepresence Lectures. If you need additional help, please visit our contact page.

Telepresence Lectures serves three primary audiences: university students seeking supplementary resources for their coursework, educators looking for quality teaching materials, and self-directed learners pursuing knowledge independently. The content is structured for anyone with sufficient mathematical maturity and motivation to engage with university-level material.

Each course contains multiple lectures organized in logical sequence. Every lecture clearly states its learning objectives and prerequisites. Courses build progressively from foundational concepts to advanced applications. We recommend following the lecture sequence in order, though experienced learners may choose to skip material they already understand.

Yes. Educators are welcome to reference, link to, or incorporate this content into their teaching with appropriate attribution. Students may cite this material in their academic work. The goal is maximum educational impact through unrestricted access. We only ask that you maintain academic integrity in how you use the material.

The current catalogue represents a carefully curated foundation across physics, mathematics, and computer science. Additional courses may be added in the future based on demand and available resources. The focus remains on quality over quantity, ensuring every lecture meets our standards for academic rigor and pedagogical clarity.

If you identify an error in course content, please contact us through the contact page with specific details about the issue. Include the course code, lecture number, and a description of the error. We take content accuracy seriously and will review and correct legitimate errors promptly.

This platform works on any modern web browser and device. The site is built with standard web technologies and requires no plugins or special software. It works equally well on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile devices. An internet connection is required to access content, though pages load quickly even on slower connections.
